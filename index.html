07 / QUESTIONS

Before you come in.

Do you take walk-ins?
Yes, walk-ins are welcome. Calling ahead on (636) 980-0494 guarantees a slot, and consultations are always free.
What are your hours?
12:30am to 8:00pm Monday through Saturday. Closed Sundays.
How old do I have to be?
18. Missouri law sets the minimum age for tattooing and government-issued photo ID is checked at the appointment.
What styles do you do?
Both artists between them cover fine line, bold traditional, colour and black & grey. Tell us the idea and it goes to whoever suits it best.
Do you do touch-ups on work from elsewhere?
Yes — touch-up work is offered on existing tattoos whether or not they were done here.
Where can I see your work?
Healed work, flash sheets and current openings are posted on Instagram at @rooster_ink_tattoo.
